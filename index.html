About Ziwani Manor

Ziwani Manor - The Most Recommended Hidden Gem

Ziwani Manor is an ideal getaway for people looking for an out of town experience. Spacious grounds camping & for your team buildings & other out of town corporate events. We have a restaurant, fully stocked bar and accommodation. We are located in Ngenda on Bobs Harries Rd off exit 16 of Thika Superhighway.
